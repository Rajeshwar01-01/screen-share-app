Connection steps and troubleshooting
  1. Open this page on both devices. On the sharer, choose a screen, window or tab and approve the browser prompt.
  2. Copy the sharer's offer into the viewer page and select Create answer. Send the resulting answer privately to the sharer.
  3. Paste the answer on the sharer page and choose Connect viewer. Keep both pages open until the session ends.
  4. If connection setup fails, retry on another network. This demo does not include a TURN service or automatic signaling server. Browser screen capture typically requires HTTPS, except on localhost.
